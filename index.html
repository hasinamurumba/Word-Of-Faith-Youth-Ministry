About our youth ministry

We are the Word Of Faith Bethesda Youth Ministry in Webuye. We help young people grow in God's Word, prayer, worship and fellowship.

Our focus

Bible study, prayer, worship, discipleship, fellowship and youth outreach.

Our heart

We encourage young people to surrender their lives to God and use their gifts for His glory.

Our challenge

Stay spiritually awake, remain faithful under pressure, resist compromise and live for God's purpose.
